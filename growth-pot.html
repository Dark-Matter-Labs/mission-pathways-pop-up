The Growth Pot

A continuity engine for the gaps between what's proven and what's next.

Dark Matter Labs works at the frontier of systemic change — in food systems, governance, the built environment and supply chains. The hardest moments in that work are rarely the big grants or the early ideas. They're the gaps in between: when a piece of work is ready to move, but the partnership or the funding hasn't caught up yet. The Growth Pot is how we close that gap, fast. We'd like to build it together with foundations who already trust what we do.

The Problem

Work doesn't stall for lack of ideas.

Across DM's workstreams, the same pattern recurs. A piece of work reaches a real inflection point — partners engaged, direction proven, the political and financial conditions almost ready — and then it waits. Not because the thinking is missing, but because there's no vehicle built for that particular, awkward moment: too developed for early curiosity funding, too early for the next institutional partner to commit.

DM has learned, project after project, that work naturally context-switches — it moves to wherever the conditions are right to continue. Someone has to hold the thread while that happens. That's the gap we call continuity, and it's what the Growth Pot exists to close.

The Mechanism

A fast, disciplined bridge — not a grant.

The Growth Pot is DM's internal fund for exactly these moments, seeded from our own core funding. It's small by design, and it moves fast.

1

Lightweight application

Any DM team can put a project forward at a live inflection point. The application is deliberately thin — enough to judge whether conditions are genuinely ripe, not a full proposal.

2

A fast judgment call

Two internal sponsors review it against one question: can a modest, well-timed investment credibly bridge this to its next phase.

3

Catalytic investment

Typically £25,000, paid at DM's standard day rate — not a discounted one — and released at the moment it can do the most good. Sometimes it's spent within weeks.

4

A conversion window

Within roughly 12–18 months, the aim is a four-times multiplier in follow-on partnership value. The timeline bends to the work — the discipline of the target is what keeps the fund honest.

4–5× the multiplier DM targets in follow-on partnership value for every Growth Pot investment — the internal bar for deciding a bridge is worth building.

The Proposition

Grow the pot with us

We're inviting a small group of foundations who already know DM's work — who've watched us spend money well, seen the depth of the partnerships we build, and understand first-hand how thin the funding is for work this far ahead of the curve — to use that trust as infrastructure. Pool into the Growth Pot, and DM can move in weeks, not funding cycles, to plug the gaps that matter.

The Direction of Travel

A staged pathway, not a single pot

We're designing the Growth Pot as the first rung of a longer ladder — closer to how early-stage capital works than to a conventional grant cycle, but built for public value rather than a market return.

Rung 1 · Live

Growth Pot

£25–50k, DM's own core funding plus co-funders. Proves whether the conditions are ripe.

Rung 2 · Emerging

Proof of traction

A demonstrated multiplier, deep partnerships, or clear signs of scalability — the gateway to the next stage.

Rung 3 · In design

A larger, longer vehicle

A more mature fund — potentially a dedicated tooling fund — for initiatives that have proved themselves ready to go further.
